# Shadow policy evaluation: `shadowv1-20261028T1605Z`

This report covers the fresh controlled scenario published on 28 October 2026. It used both running
Flink jobs, the deterministic router, the CPU LightGBM shadow scorer, the Go correlator, the dry-run
investigation worker, and `fraud-action-policy-v1`. All five Kafka consumer groups returned to zero lag.

## Latest outcome per transaction

The fixture produced 16 logical payment transactions and 24 Kafka shadow-decision events. The extra
events are expected: investigation request/completion data created later correlated assessment revisions.
Policy output did not depend on that Claude audit context.

| `APPROVED` | 9 | 2 | 0 | 11 |

Shadow distribution: 9 approved, 4 challenged and 3 blocked. There were 14 agreements and two
disagreements with the immutable verdict. Agreement is a comparison metric, not fraud accuracy.

The ML-driven differences were:

- `controlled-shadowv1-20261028T1605Z-hopping-3`: `APPROVED` to `CHALLENGED`, probability
  `0.25447106624911336`.
- `controlled-shadowv1-20261028T1605Z-velocity-3`: `APPROVED` to `CHALLENGED`, probability
  `0.5330738091656015`.

The three deterministic hard blocks remained blocked at probabilities `0.3202713149435478`,
`0.5232055043638224`, and `0.5250232204384847`. The dry-run investigation recommendation was retained
only as audit context and did not override those controls.

## Partial inputs and latency

The engine recorded 18 `AWAITING_INPUTS` revisions and published no decision for them. Later revisions
made all 16 transactions evaluable. Latest-per-transaction pure policy latency was 0.003425 ms mean,
0.002105 ms median, 0.007521 ms p95 and 0.012709 ms maximum. These figures exclude Kafka, PostgreSQL,
Flink, model inference and network time; they are not an end-to-end payment latency claim.

## Accuracy limitation

This controlled stream does not attach trustworthy transaction-level ground-truth fraud labels. False
positive and false negative rates therefore cannot be computed for this run. The LightGBM model and its
0.24 validation threshold came from synthetic data; this report does not establish calibration,
production accuracy, fairness, robustness, customer impact or financial value.
